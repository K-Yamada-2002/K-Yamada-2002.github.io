<p class="lead narrow">
  I am interested in ergodic theory and Diophantine approximation. My current work concerns the Lagrange and Markov spectra, particularly the intervals they contain.
</p>

<div class="entry-list">
  <section class="entry">
    <h2>Verifying Hall’s ray and constructing interior points below it</h2>
    <p>I used Schecker’s method to give a computer-assisted proof that the Lagrange and Markov spectra contain the half-line beginning at Freiman’s constant (approximately 4.52783).</p>
    <p>This proof led me to show that both spectra contain the closed intervals [4.51104888, 4.52] and [4.52173315, 4.52762938], both below Hall’s ray. To my knowledge, this is the first explicit construction of interior points below the ray.</p>
    <p><a href="https://github.com/K-Yamada-2002/verify-freiman-constant">Proofs and verification code on GitHub</a></p>
